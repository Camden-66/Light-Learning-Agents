Light Learning Agents

You are outside a room.
Peek a few times, then bet when the light is most likely on.

This page talks to the canonical RoomEnv in the repo — 32 time slots, a hidden peak, binary on/off draws. Play as the agent, then run oracle MLE on the same episode, and compare with a small REINFORCE demonstration.

Room rules

A day is 32 slots. The hidden peak θ is drawn from 4–27. Closer slots are more likely on:

P(on | t, θ) = 0.05 + 0.90 · exp(−(t − θ)² / 18)

Each look costs one unit of budget. Repeats are allowed. When the budget is gone, click a slot as θ̂. Score is |θ − θ̂| (lower is better). θ is private until you commit.

What the benchmark baselines do

Oracle MLE does not adapt: it spends the budget on i.i.d. uniform slots, then maximizes the true Bernoulli likelihood over θ ∈ {4,…,27} (smallest candidate on a tie). It knows the formula, so it is an oracle-information reference, not an information-matched human baseline.

PPO never sees the formula. It only sees on-counts, visit-counts, remaining budget, and a commit flag. Looks give reward 0; the only reward is −|θ − θ̂| / 31 at commit. It learns a search policy, not the curve itself.

The quick interactive trainer below is REINFORCE, not PPO. Its smoke-sample numbers are illustrative and must not be reported as official benchmark results.
